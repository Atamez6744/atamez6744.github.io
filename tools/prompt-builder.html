Prompt Builder

Most bad AI answers come from one-line prompts. This builder walks you through the four parts of a prompt that works: Goal, Context, Source, and Expectations. It scores your prompt as you type and shows you how to iterate.

🧑‍🍳
Prompting is like briefing a brilliant new teammate on their first day. They're smart, but they don't know your project, your audience, or what "good" looks like. The more you tell them, the better the first draft, and you'd never expect a new hire to nail it without one round of feedback.

Build your prompt

Start from a template or a blank page. Every field is editable.

G

Start with a clear action verb: draft, summarize, compare, brainstorm, rewrite.

C

Who is the audience? What's the situation? What role should the AI play?

S

Point to the file, email, meeting, or data. In Copilot, type "/" to reference a file.

E

Format, length, tone, and what to avoid. Bullets? A table? Under 200 words?
