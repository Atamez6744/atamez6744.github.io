AI Use Case Prioritizer

Every organization has a list of 40 "we should use AI for…" ideas. This tool helps you find the five worth starting with: score each idea, tune the weights to your priorities, and see where it lands on an Impact vs. Ease map.

Think of it like house hunting. You don't buy the first house you see. You decide what matters (location, price, space), weight it, and compare. Same with AI: the shiniest idea isn't always the right first move.

How the math works: Impact combines Business Value and Reach. Ease combines Data Readiness, Feasibility, low Risk, and low Change Effort. Each factor is weighted by your sliders and normalized to 0–100. Change Effort is in here on purpose. In my experience, the people side is the factor teams most often forget to score.
